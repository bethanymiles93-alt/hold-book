# Hold principles

## We assume you care

Silence is not treated as proof of indifference.

## Wellbeing before engagement

Hold does not optimise for daily use, streaks, screen time or habitual checking.

## No guilt as a growth mechanism

No shame-based copy, red badges for emotional tasks, escalating reminders or “you missed” language.

## Choice before automation

AI may draft and suggest. The user reviews, edits, selects recipients and initiates every message.

## Explain what will happen

Before an action, show who receives it, what is shared, whether anything is stored and whether AI is involved.

## Low capacity is the design baseline

The core journey must remain usable when attention, memory, initiation, emotional regulation and physical energy are impaired.

## Relationships before metrics

One useful interaction that protects a friendship is more valuable than repeated app engagement.

## Rest is not failure

Hold never frames recuperation as falling behind.

## Returning matters

The product is not complete when a Hold is sent. Reconnection deserves equal care.

## Privacy is emotional safety

Collect the minimum information necessary and provide clear deletion and consent controls.

## Human language, not clinical theatre

Use warm, direct language. Do not imply medical authority.

## Pause or cancel without friction

Commercial design must match the product’s values.

## Things Hold will never do

- Sell private message content
- Automatically message personal contacts
- Create friendship or reliability scores
- Punish inactivity
- Make cancellation deliberately difficult
- Pretend AI understands a relationship better than the people in it
- Diagnose, treat or provide crisis care
