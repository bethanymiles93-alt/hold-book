# Mission and vision

## Mission

Help people protect the relationships they care about when their capacity to communicate temporarily disappears.

## Vision

A world in which periods of illness, grief, burnout or overwhelm do not automatically become periods of misunderstanding, shame and relationship loss.

## What Hold is

- A relationship-preservation tool
- A low-capacity communication aid
- A calm bridge between silence and reconnection
- A user-controlled assistant for drafting and sharing honest messages

## What Hold is not

- A therapy service
- A crisis service
- A diagnostic tool
- A social network
- A productivity tracker
- A substitute for human care
- An app that speaks to contacts without explicit user action

## Positioning

Avoid framing Hold around one diagnosis. ADHD, PMDD, depression, anxiety, chronic illness, fatigue, grief, caring responsibilities and major life events can all reduce communication capacity.

The shared problem is not diagnosis. It is the mismatch between **care** and **capacity**.

## One-line descriptions

**Primary**

> Hold helps you protect important relationships when you do not have the capacity to communicate normally.

**Short**

> A gentler way to go quiet and come back.

**Functional**

> Draft and share a simple message before you withdraw, then get help reconnecting when you are ready.
