# Trauma-informed design

Hold is not a trauma-treatment product, but trauma-informed principles provide useful safeguards for users who may feel ashamed, frightened, exhausted or dysregulated.

## Relevant principles

- Safety
- Trust and transparency
- Choice
- Collaboration
- Empowerment
- Respect for context and difference

## Product interpretation

### Safety
No unexpected sending, hidden recipients or ambiguous sharing.

### Transparency
Explain AI use, storage and message handling in plain language.

### Choice
Allow editing, declining, skipping and deleting. Do not force disclosure.

### Collaboration
Co-design with intended users and include recipient perspectives.

### Empowerment
Treat the user as capable of making decisions even when capacity is limited.

## Interface implications

- Never auto-send.
- Never use a countdown.
- Confirm recipients and text before sharing.
- Allow leaving without losing work.
- Avoid forcing a reason or diagnosis.
- Distinguish Hold from emergency support.
- Make help links available without turning every screen into a crisis screen.
