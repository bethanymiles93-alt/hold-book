# Research map

## Core research questions

1. What causes communication withdrawal during periods of reduced capacity?
2. How does prolonged silence affect trust and relationship perception?
3. Which interface choices reduce cognitive and emotional effort?
4. How can Hold apply trauma-informed principles without presenting as clinical care?
5. What accessibility standards apply to low-capacity interactions?
6. What data is truly necessary?
7. What are the risks of AI-assisted personal messages?
8. Which outcomes can be measured without turning relationships into scores?

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer:

1. Standards and regulators
2. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
3. Peer-reviewed primary studies
4. Reputable charities and clinical organisations
5. Structured user research
6. Community discussion for hypothesis generation
7. Founder insight
