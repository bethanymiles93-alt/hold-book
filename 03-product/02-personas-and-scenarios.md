# Personas and scenarios

These are proto-personas requiring research.

## Episodic capacity loss

A person whose capacity changes due to PMDD, depression, anxiety, migraine, chronic illness, pain or fatigue.

**Need:** communicate without explaining a diagnosis every time.

## Overwhelmed or burnt out

A person facing work overload, caring responsibilities or a major life event.

**Need:** set expectations with several people.

## Grieving

A person receiving many messages while having little capacity to reply.

**Need:** reassure people and create space.

## ADHD communication freeze

A person who delays a reply, feels shame and becomes less able to return.

**Need:** send an imperfect bridge message quickly.

## Recipient

A friend, relative or colleague who receives a Hold.

**Need:** understand what silence means, what is expected and whether to worry.
