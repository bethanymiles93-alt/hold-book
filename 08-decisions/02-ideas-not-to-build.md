# Ideas not to build

These ideas are excluded unless new evidence overturns the decision.

- Streaks
- Relationship health scores
- Automatic WhatsApp replies
- Reading private conversations
- Public social profiles
- “Friends waiting” reminders
- Recipient open tracking
- Diagnosis requirements
- Mandatory return dates
- Gamified recovery
- Weekly subscription
- Lifetime access before unit economics are known
- AI sending messages without review
