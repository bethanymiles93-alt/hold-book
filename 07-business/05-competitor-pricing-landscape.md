# Competitor pricing landscape

**Status:** Founder-supplied market scan; prices require verification before publication or investor use.  
**Purpose:** Establish broad willingness-to-pay ranges and identify what users are paying for.

## Pricing categories

| Category | Illustrative monthly range | Main value sold |
|---|---:|---|
| Planning and self-guided tools | £5–£15 | Structure, routines, meditation and tracking |
| Interactive support and coaching | £20–£50 | Personalisation, accountability and human support |
| Clinical teletherapy | £260–£400 | Access to licensed professionals |

## ADHD and neurodivergent support

Examples discussed by the founder include:

- Inflow: self-guided ADHD education, with a higher-priced coaching tier
- Tiimo: visual planning and support for time blindness
- EndeavorOTC: game-based digital treatment for attention

The main pricing lesson is that human coaching increases the acceptable price substantially. Software-only products are generally priced much lower.

## Depression and anxiety support

Examples discussed include:

- Headspace
- Calm
- Wysa
- Woebot

These products often justify subscriptions through large content libraries, repeated daily use, personalisation or optional human support.

## Relationship support

Examples discussed include:

- Paired
- Gottman Card Decks
- Agapé
- Daylio
- MyTherapy
- Talkspace Couples Therapy

These products demonstrate willingness to pay for relationship support, but most depend on ongoing engagement, prompts, tracking or clinical care.

## Donation-supported comparison

ScreenZen provides its core functionality free and relies on optional tips rather than subscriptions or advertising.

This model demonstrates that a respectful, mission-led product can generate goodwill and voluntary support. It does not yet prove that a donation-only model would cover Hold's likely recurring costs, particularly AI, backend, support and security costs.

## Hold's position

Hold's MVP is deliberately narrow:

- one core low-capacity communication action
- optional AI help with wording
- one return and reconnection action

It should therefore launch below broad content platforms, coaching programmes and relationship subscriptions.

## Pricing conclusion

A lower introductory price is more proportionate to the first version and reduces the barrier to trusting an unfamiliar product.

Recommended initial hypothesis:

| Tier | Price |
|---|---:|
| Free | £0 |
| Monthly | £2.99–£3.99 |
| Annual | £19.99–£24.99 |
| Supporter tier | Optional, later |

The launch test should compare £2.99 and £3.99 rather than assume £4.99.

## Important limitation

Competitor prices vary by country, platform, promotion and date. All quoted prices must be rechecked directly before they are used externally.
