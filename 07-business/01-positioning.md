# Positioning

## Category

Hold sits between:

- communication tools
- wellbeing tools
- relationship support
- low-capacity accessibility

It should not be positioned as therapy, productivity or a generic AI writer.

## Primary promise

> Protect the relationships you care about when you do not have the capacity to communicate normally.

## Differentiators

- Designed specifically for low-capacity moments
- Covers both going quiet and returning
- No guilt-based engagement
- Recipient-aware
- Diagnosis-free
- Explicit privacy and AI boundaries
- Calm, accessible interface

## Potential audiences

- People with episodic mental or physical health conditions
- Neurodivergent people experiencing communication freeze
- People in grief
- Carers
- People in burnout or major life transitions
- Loved ones seeking a clearer signal

## Risks

- The name “Hold” may be difficult to own in search.
- The product could be mistaken for crisis support.
- Over-medicalised messaging may narrow adoption.
- Very broad positioning may make launch messaging vague.

## Recommendation

Launch around one clear situation—“when you care but cannot reply”—while keeping the product open to multiple causes.
