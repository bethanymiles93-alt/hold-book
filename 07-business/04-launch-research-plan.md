# Launch research plan

## Phase 1 — problem validation

Interview 12–20 people across:

- episodic mental health
- chronic illness and fatigue
- ADHD communication freeze
- grief
- caring responsibilities
- recipient/loved-one perspective

## Phase 2 — message testing

Test:

- “Create a Hold”
- “Going quiet”
- “Return from Hold”
- “Then rest”
- “Your people know”
- “Relationship preservation”

## Phase 3 — prototype usability

Observe whether users can:

- understand the purpose
- choose recipients
- create a message
- edit it
- understand what will happen
- return later without feeling behind

## Phase 4 — pricing

Use real choice tests rather than asking only “would you pay?”

Compare:

- free only
- £2.99 monthly / £19.99 yearly
- £3.99 monthly / £24.99 yearly
- one-off packs
- scholarship/access option
- easy pause

## Early success measures

- Percentage completing a Hold
- Percentage who say they would otherwise have stayed silent
- Self-reported reduction in guilt
- Recipient understanding
- Return-message completion
- Trust in privacy and AI
- Support burden
- Paid conversion without pressure tactics
