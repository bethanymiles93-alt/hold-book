# Clinical safety reviewer leads

Research leads for the clinical safety review Hold needs before safeguarding features can go live — see the blocked task in `08-decisions/04-open-questions.md`, "Safeguarding trigger logic and wording," and the earlier reviewer-hiring research in `06-privacy-security/03-safeguarding.md`, "Finding a clinical reviewer." **Not a decision, not a confirmed vendor relationship — leads and contacts only**, gathered while researching Calm Harm as a comparable app.

**Status: none of these leads have been contacted.** This file is a starting point for outreach, not a shortlist of confirmed options.

## mHabitat — important correction to how this should be understood

mHabitat conducted Calm Harm's (stem4's self-harm support app) clinical safety review, but this happened specifically as part of the **NHS England-funded "Digital Development Lab" programme**, run in collaboration with stem4 and design agency HMA. It was **not** a standalone commercial service Calm Harm simply purchased off the shelf — it was access gained through that specific NHS programme relationship.

**Do not treat mHabitat as a ready-to-book vendor.** Honest status: mHabitat is a verified real example of *who can do this kind of review*, not a confirmed engagement path. Two things worth checking, neither confirmed:
(a) whether the Digital Development Lab programme is still running and open to new applicants;
(b) whether mHabitat takes independent commercial engagements outside that programme.

## Commercial CSO / clinical-safety consultancies researched

Safehand, DigiSafe, 8fold, DPM Digital Health, BMS Digital Safety, Innovate Health Consulting, Kaleidoscope Consultants, NECS — all found via web search, **no confirmed pricing for any**.

**Honest caveat:** most of these are built around ongoing DCB0129/DTAC compliance retainers for NHS suppliers (e.g. DigiSafe's advertised starting price of £999/month) — a different scale and service model than Hold likely needs pre-launch, which is a one-off scoped review of the safeguarding detection logic and wording, not ongoing NHS-supplier compliance.

**Recommended approach when contacting any of these:** ask explicitly whether they offer a one-off scoped review outside their standard retainer model, rather than assuming their advertised service fits Hold's actual need.
