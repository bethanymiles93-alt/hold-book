# NHS / ORCHA accreditation pathways — research reference

Background research only. Not a decision, not an open question requiring action — kept for reference if/when accreditation is actively pursued post-MVP.

## Applicability

- NHS DCB0129/DCB0160 are NOT mandated for Hold. They apply to technologies "used to influence, support, manage real-time or near-real-time direct care" within NHS/adult social care deployment. Hold, as a standalone consumer app, does not meet this threshold. Confirmed via NHS England's own developer guidance and applicability step-by-step tool.
- ORCHA accreditation is NOT mandatory, "strongly encouraged," commonly requested during NHS procurement or app-library inclusion. ~4-6 weeks process once submitted. No public pricing found — subscription/quote-based; requires direct enquiry.
- ORCHA reviews against four pillars: Data & Privacy, Clinical/Professional Assurance, Evidence, and Usability & Accessibility (130+ elements assessed).

## CSO training (if ever pursued)

NHS Digital Clinical Safety training, confirmed current pricing:
- Essentials: free (NHS/gov.uk/hscni.net email), £35+VAT otherwise
- Intermediate: free (NHS/gov.uk/hscni.net email), £50+VAT otherwise — prerequisite for Practitioner
- Practitioner: £475 (NHS) / £625 (commercial) — the course that builds toward CSO-capable status
- Digital Safety in Practice (optional): £95

Note: training confers competence, not independence — a family member serving as CSO for a founder's own product raises a separate conflict-of-interest question, unresolved, worth a direct question to a solicitor or training body rather than assumed either way.

## Comparable app: Calm Harm (stem4)

- NHS App Library listing came via the NHS-funded "Digital Development Lab" programme (delivered with mHabitat and agency HMA), not a standing purchasable service. Worth checking whether this programme is still open to new applicants before treating mHabitat as a direct commercial vendor.
- Reached NHS App Library status after ~2+ years of live usage (launched April 2017, in Digital Development Lab process by ~2019), not a pre-launch step.
- Public safeguarding policy, privacy policy, and accessibility statement are all published separately from internal clinical safety documentation (Clinical Risk Management Plan, Clinical Safety Case Report, Hazard Log — available on request only, not public).
- Full FAQ, safeguarding policy, and privacy policy at calmharm.stem4.org.uk — useful structural templates for Hold's own future public-facing documents.

## Independent app review alternative

Healthify (NZ) and ORCHA (UK) both run independent, charity/commercially-backed health app review libraries. ORCHA is the directly relevant UK option. Worth approaching once evidence-gathering (see `08-decisions/04-open-questions.md`, the beta/pilot and "did this help?" entries) produces real outcome data to submit.
