# Notification policy

## Principle

A notification is a demand on attention. Hold must justify every demand.

## Default

No engagement notifications by default.

## Permitted categories

- User-requested reminders
- Essential security and account notices
- Necessary service-delivery updates

## Prohibited patterns

- “Your friends are waiting”
- “You have been quiet for 7 days”
- streaks
- escalating urgency
- fake concern used to drive opens
- repeated prompts after dismissal
- red badges for emotional actions
- sensitive lock-screen text by default

## Safer copy

> A reminder you asked for: your draft is here when you need it.

> You asked Hold to check in today. Nothing is overdue.
