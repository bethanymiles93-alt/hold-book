# Core journeys

## Home

### Emotional goal
“I am safe here. I do not need to catch up.”

### Primary actions
- **Create a Hold**
- **Return from Hold**

### Avoid
- activity feeds
- overdue tasks
- streaks
- social comparison
- busy dashboards

## Create a Hold

1. Who needs to know?
2. What do you need them to understand?
3. Review recipients and message together.
4. Share using the platform share sheet.
5. Completion: **Ready. Then rest.**

Suggested intents:

- I need quiet
- I am unwell
- Life is a lot right now
- I need some time
- Write my own

## Return from Hold

1. Who would you like to come back to?
2. How much do you want to say?
3. Review and share.

Suggested return styles:

- Just open the door
- Acknowledge the silence
- Explain a little
- Write my own

Completion:

> That was enough.

## Technical wording rule

Native share sheets may not confirm delivery. Use “sent” only when the platform can truthfully confirm sending.
